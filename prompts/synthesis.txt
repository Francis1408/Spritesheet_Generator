SOURCES
1. POSITION OBSERVATIONS — independent analyses of the front, back, left, and
   right views (some may be missing).
2. FULL SHEET OBSERVATION — one analysis of all views together.
3. COLOR PROFILE — pixel-measured color percentages per view and region
   (upper / middle / lower). Computed from the image, not a model opinion.

SOURCE PRIORITY
- Exact color and exact garment of a specific region: POSITION OBSERVATIONS.
- Silhouette, gender/race shape evidence, weapons, back-only or side-only items
  (capes, cloaks, hair length), and whether two views show the SAME object:
  FULL SHEET OBSERVATION.
- Skin-vs-clothing ambiguity and color consistency: COLOR PROFILE. It overrides a
  verbal "clothing" or "armor" label when the numbers clearly disagree.
- If the full sheet merges torso and legs into one description, split it at the
  boundary the position observations use.

MERGING
- Check all available views for every field. Combine compatible detail from
  minor views (front reports a belt, right adds a gold buckle → keep both).
- If two or more sources report the same feature in different words, treat it as
  strong evidence even when each was rated "inferred" or "guess".
- Coverage rule: every distinct item named in any source must end up in exactly
  one category, or be explicitly rejected in that category's analysis. Do not let
  an item silently disappear.
- A field marked NOT_VISIBLE or UNKNOWN by one source is NOT evidence of absence.
  Fall back to the other sources before concluding a feature is missing.
- Mark a field unsupported only when no source mentions it at all.

COLOR PROFILE READING
- A color strong in "upper" across multiple views → likely skin tone.
- A color spread across "middle" in most views but weak elsewhere → likely the
  main torso garment.
- A color mainly in "lower" across views → likely legs or footwear.
- A color heavy in ONE view and ONE region only → a feature visible from that
  angle only (cape, back item, side-slung weapon).
- Colors below the measurement threshold are omitted from the profile. Absence
  from the profile is NOT evidence a small item does not exist — a thin trim
  line, a buckle, or an eye color can be real and still fall below it.

SKIN
Skin may be blue, green, purple, red, gray, black, white, or pale — not only
human tones. Compare exposed regions (face, neck, hands, arms, legs, feet).
- Gray SKIN: soft rounded seamless shading on organic parts, consistent across views.
- Gray ARMOR: rigid plates, seams, rivets, sharp metallic highlights.
- Pale CLOTHING: bounded by a differently-colored face or hands at a neckline or cuff.
- Pale SKIN: the same tone continues unbroken into face, neck, or hands.

SKIN-VS-GARMENT CHECK for body and lower
Override a garment label to bare skin ("bare chest" / "bare legs") only when BOTH
hold: (a) the color profile shows the established skin color clearly DOMINANT in
that region, and (b) no garment of a different color is reported for that region
by two or more sources. Remember the arms occupy the middle band, so some skin
color there is normal on a clothed character — mere presence is not dominance.
Keep the garment when a structural boundary is reported: a collar or neckline in a
different color, buttons, a seam, a cuff, a waistband, or a fold/wrinkle pattern.

GENDER AND RACE
- Narrower waist plus a rounded chest curve breaking the torso outline, in
  multiple views → female.
- Straight or block-shaped torso, broad square shoulders, flat chest line, little
  waist narrowing → male.
- Inconsistent or ambiguous silhouette across views → omit gender entirely.
- Pointed/elongated ears with human-toned skin → elf. Pointed ears with green or
  gray-green skin → goblin, or orc if bulky and muscular. Tusks, horns, or an
  unusual skin color with a stocky build → orc or another fantasy race. No
  supporting anatomical evidence → humanoid.

FULL-BODY GARMENTS
If one garment (robe, dress, cloak) covers torso and legs with no visible break in
any view, describe it once under body as full-length, and state under lower that
the legs and feet are not visible. Never invent bare legs or pants.
